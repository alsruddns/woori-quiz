The quiz service contains general knowledge, geography, science, history, math, language, IT, memory, speed/reaction, and entertainment experiences. Official exam rules and curriculum-aligned content are excluded from the initial release. The fun category is entertainment and does not diagnose personality or health.

Current rollout state: only Korean and English routes are public. Other locale routes return not found while their question banks are incomplete. Thirty-eight static quiz entries remain closed while their question sets are authored and reviewed.
